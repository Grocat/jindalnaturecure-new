What Makes Our Campus Green?

At Jindal Naturecure Institute, nature is not just a backdrop; it is a core part of therapy. Our campus is home to over 3,500+ trees across 56 clusters, carefully preserved and nurtured by our horticulture team.

From medicinal plants to shaded healing spaces, every element is designed to align with the principles of natural healing.
